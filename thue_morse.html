A visualisation of the Thue-Morse sequence that was inspired by episode 140 of "A Problem Squared". Pick how many players there are (up to 14). Each circle is a player, A at the left, and choosing a scale maps them onto notes ascending from middle C. Press play and the sequence is generated turn by turn, for as long as you like, with each player dipping down the track as its note sounds. You can also play the plain scale for comparison. This is forked from https://github.com/damienkee/thue-morese_visualiser.
